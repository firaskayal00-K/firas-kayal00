Our Story

Made slowly, on purpose — furniture meant to outlast trends.

Wren & Oak began as a small neighborhood workshop with one belief: furniture should be made from real materials, built to be repaired rather than replaced, and priced honestly. Today we work with independent makers and small mills to bring that same philosophy to every piece we sell — whether it's crafted in-house or sourced from a maker we trust.

What We Believe

Quality over quantity

We'd rather sell you one chair you'll keep for twenty years than five you'll replace in two. Every product page lists real materials and care instructions, not marketing language.

Where It's Made

Small workshops, honest sourcing

Our wood pieces are built by a family-run mill using FSC-certified timber. Upholstered pieces are sewn locally, in small batches, so we can catch quality issues before they reach you.
